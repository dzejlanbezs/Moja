Loading screens
===============

Drop a short clip here and it plays instead of the built-in spinning logo:

  intro.mp4     the splash when the site opens or is refreshed
  sports.mp4    the splash when the sportsbook opens

.mp4 and .webm both work. Both screens last two seconds, so a two second clip
fits exactly — anything longer is cut off, anything shorter freezes on its last
frame. The clip plays muted with no controls, so keep the message visual.

Leave the folder empty and the built-in animation stays: the logo for the site,
the gold sports mark for the sportsbook.
