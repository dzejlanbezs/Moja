Originals tile backgrounds
==========================

Drop an image here to replace the background art of a game tile in the lobby.
The badge, game name and player count stay on top.

  blackholes.jpg    blackjack.jpg    coinflip.jpg    dice.jpg      digdig.jpg
  keno.jpg          limbo.jpg        mines.jpg       plinko.jpg    wheel.jpg

Any of .jpg .jpeg .png .webp .avif works and names are matched loosely, so
black-holes.png or Dig Dig.webp are fine. Whatever is missing keeps the current
built-in artwork.

Tiles are portrait, so 600x800 (3:4) looks best. These images are also used as
the little thumbnail in the Live Wins strip and the bet feed.
