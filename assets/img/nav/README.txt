Casino and Sports tiles
=======================

The two tiles at the top of the left rail take their picture from here:

  casino.png    sports.png

Any of .jpg .jpeg .png .webp .avif works. They are shown as squares, so around
160x160 or larger looks best. The icon sits on top of a dark scrim, and the
selected tile is tinted green, so both keep working over any artwork.

Leave a file out and that tile keeps its plain background. The pictures only
show while the rail is narrow — once it is expanded, or open on a phone, the
entries read as plain rows with their names.
