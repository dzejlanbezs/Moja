Live Wins thumbnails
====================

The square next to every win in the Live Wins strip. Name the file after the
game it belongs to:

  dice.png       limbo.png       coinflip.png    mines.png     blackjack.png
  keno.png       digdig.png      blackholes.png  plinko.png    wheel.png

Any of .jpg .jpeg .png .webp .avif works. They are shown as small rounded
squares, so 128x128 or larger is plenty.

Anything missing here falls back to the tile art in assets/img/games, and then
to the built-in badge. Sportsbook wins keep their own mark from
assets/img/sports/logo.
