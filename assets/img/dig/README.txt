Dig Dig artwork
===============

Two files replace the built-in gold and rock:

  gold.png    what a safe tile turns into
  rock.png    what the tile that ends the round turns into

Any of .jpg .jpeg .png .webp .avif works. Tiles are about 48px tall, so 128x128
or larger is plenty, and transparency looks best. Leave one out and that half
keeps the built-in icon.
