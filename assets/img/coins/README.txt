Currency icons
==============

Drop an icon here to replace the coloured letter circle next to a currency in
the Cashier, on both the Deposit and the Withdraw tab. Name the file after the
ticker:

  usdt.png    usdc.png    eth.png    btc.png    sol.png

Any of .jpg .jpeg .png .webp .avif works. Square images look best, 128x128 or
larger, and transparency is fine. Whatever is missing keeps the current circle.
