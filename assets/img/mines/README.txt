Mines artwork
=============

Two files replace the built-in gem and mine:

  gem.png     what a safe tile turns into
  mine.png    what the tile you lost on turns into

Any of .jpg .jpeg .png .webp .avif works. Tiles are square and about 70x70 on
screen, so 160x160 or larger is plenty, and a transparent PNG or WEBP sits best
on the coloured tile. Leave one out and that half keeps the built-in icon.
