Trending Now background
=======================

Drop one image here and it becomes the background of all three Trending Now
cards on the Sports page. The filename does not matter — the first image in
this folder is used, so `trending.png`, `mlb-night.jpg` or anything else works.

  .jpg  .jpeg  .png  .webp  .avif

Wide artwork looks best, around 900x260 or larger. The team names and kick-off
time are drawn on top of a dark scrim, so a busy image still reads well.
Leave the folder empty and the built-in purple gradient stays.
