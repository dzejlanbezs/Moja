Match page backgrounds
======================

The picture behind the two teams at the top of a match page, one per sport.
Name the file after the sport:

  soccer.png        tennis.png         basketball.png     baseball.png
  volleyball.png    handball.png       ice-hockey.png     american-football.png
  e-sports.png      mma-ufc.png        boxing.png         snooker.png
  cricket.png       darts.png          table-tennis.png   badminton.png
  rugby-union.png   rugby-league.png   australian-rules.png
  futsal.png        horse-racing.png   greyhounds.png     bowls.png
  gaelic-sports.png floorball.png      beach-volleyball.png
  water-polo.png    squash.png

Any of .jpg .jpeg .png .webp .avif works, and the name is matched loosely, so
`Ice Hockey.jpg` or `icehockey.png` land in the same place. The card is wide and
short — around 1200x300 or larger looks best.

A dark scrim is drawn over the picture so the team names, the crests and the
kick-off time stay readable. Add `default.png` and it is used for any sport that
has no file of its own; leave the folder empty and the built-in gradient stays.
